Attendance that keeps everyone in the loop.

Looper Agency Attendance is the employee attendance system of Looper Agency: clock in and out from the company or online, track working hours, book vacation, unpaid leave and days off, and see the team calendar.

  • Clock in and out from the office or online
  • Book vacation, unpaid leave and days off, with approval by your department head
  • Team calendar with public holidays, birthdays and work anniversaries
  • Timesheets and reports for managers
